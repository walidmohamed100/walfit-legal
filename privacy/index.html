2. Information we process and why

Account and sign-in information

Name, email address, phone number entered at registration, account identifier, account creation and successful-login timestamps support authentication, account management and support. Firebase Authentication handles sign-in; registration contact fields, including the entered phone number, are written to Cloud Firestore.

Passwords entered for email sign-in or reauthentication are processed by Firebase Authentication. They are not fields in WALFIT's profile documents.

Fitness and health-related information

Age, gender, height, current and starting weight, goals, activity level, training experience, home/gym setting, equipment, sleep quality, consistency, obstacles, and food allergies you enter are used to generate and maintain fitness/nutrition plans and personalize the service. Injury and chronic-condition information can remain in previously saved profiles and can inform plan guidance; the current personal-information form does not ask for these fields. Saved profiles and generated plans are stored in Firestore.

Workout and progress information

Exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, dates, durations, completion status, estimated calories and notes help record, restore and review workouts. Supported workout modes use local files; cloud-backed flows also store records in Firestore.

Nutrition information

Foods, portions, meals, water intake, nutrient totals, preferences, custom foods and recipes support meal logging, nutrition calculations and planning. Local daily drafts and cloud-backed records are used, depending on the feature.

Images and content you choose

A photo selected for a custom exercise, exercise names and notes are used to display and save custom content. Selected images are resized and can be saved as image data in Firestore and included in local workout records.

Membership and coaching information

Assigned access/package status, plans, follow-up and session records, and subscription/access metadata support membership and coaching services. These records do not, by themselves, mean that WALFIT processes card details or that a store payment is active.

Preferences and reminders

Language, reminder selections, notification identifiers and schedules, rest-timer preferences, pinned notes and weight units apply your settings and deliver reminders. These use device storage and operating-system notification services; some preferences have cloud-backed counterparts.

Security and operational information

Account-reset status and timestamps, account identifiers, app/device attestation information and network/service metadata protect accounts, prevent abuse and help prevent interrupted deletion from restoring old data. Firebase and attestation providers process information needed for those services.

Support and privacy correspondence

Contact details and message contents are used to respond to and verify requests sent to walfit2026@hotmail.com. This mailbox is personally monitored by Waleed Mohamed. Requests are reviewed manually and may require account-ownership verification; sending an email does not automatically delete an account.

Google Sign-In uses Google and Firebase authentication and provides account information such as your identifier, name and email.

3. Device storage, permissions and sharing

WALFIT uses application-private files and preferences for supported workout history, drafts, nutrition working data, restoration metadata and settings. These are separate from Firebase SDK-managed authentication state and Firestore caches. App-private storage does not mean every file is separately encrypted by WALFIT.

The app can request access to a selected photo for a custom exercise and notification permission for reminders. Permissions can be managed in device settings; denying one may limit the relevant feature.

If you choose to share a workout summary, WALFIT opens the system sharing interface. The recipient or application you select then handles the shared information under its own practices. Account deletion does not recall shared copies or remove original photo-library images.

4. Service providers and feature availability

WALFIT uses Google Firebase Authentication for account access, Cloud Firestore for cloud records, Cloud Functions for backend operations, and Firebase App Check with platform attestation services for abuse prevention. These services process information needed to provide authentication, storage, backend and security functions, including account identifiers and relevant device/network information.

Firebase Cloud Messaging is included and registered in the app. The current app configuration does not explicitly disable its default automatic initialization and registration. It may process installation identifiers, notification tokens and related device/network information. This configuration does not establish that a token was issued or a push notification was delivered on every device; actual behavior depends on device state, saved settings, platform requirements and connectivity. Cancelling local reminders does not delete Firebase installation identifiers or establish that server push has stopped.

Provider information: Firebase privacy and security and Google Privacy Policy.

The current app configuration disables the AI food scanner and the workout-completion advertising provider. This does not establish removal of information previously processed by older versions or external services. Static promotional banners and external social/messaging links may appear in the app. A service you choose to open handles your interaction under its own practices.

Authorized administrators, including coaches granted the administrator role, can read user profile documents across accounts and specified training-plan, nutrition-plan, membership and coaching records under the configured Firestore client rules. This access is not restricted to individually assigned customers. Those rules restrict direct client access to daily logs, workout history, weight logs, custom foods and recipes to the account owner; administrator status alone does not grant access to those records through the app’s client. Separate privileged backend or administrative access is governed independently. User-directed sharing, email correspondence and interactions with external services involve the recipients or providers you choose.

Support and deletion emails are handled through Microsoft Hotmail/Outlook. Microsoft processes email contents, contact details and service metadata under its Privacy Statement.

When this website is served through GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, whether or not they are signed in. See GitHub Pages data collection. These website files do not include analytics scripts, advertising scripts, cookies or a request-submission form.

5. Account deletion and Delete Data

In WALFIT, open Settings → Account & data, choose an action and complete the supported identity verification.

In-app Delete Account

The in-app server deletion flow targets your private Firestore user document and subcollections, matching shared program-reminder records, and your Firebase Authentication account. Supported local cleanup then targets the exact account on that device.

On successful server deletion, the in-app Delete Account flow also requests cancellation of all WALFIT local notifications on that device, including reminders from other WALFIT accounts previously used there. Rescheduling is suppressed until an eligible new account explicitly enables notifications with permission. This does not affect other apps or revoke OS notification permission, and does not establish that an independent push service has stopped.

A deletion request handled outside the app cannot remotely erase local files, drafts, cached data or scheduled notifications on your devices. The device cleanup described above applies to the in-app flow.

Delete Data

Delete Data resets personal profile/content and private data collections while retaining the Firebase Authentication account and sign-in information, plus specified role, package/access, subscription and billing-related metadata. It can sign you out and require fresh sign-in. It does not delete the account itself or cancel a separate billing arrangement.

Interrupted deletion and requests without the app

If deletion is interrupted, limited recovery information may remain on the device. Use the cleanup/recovery action when offered, or contact support if completion still fails.

Without app access, use the Account Deletion Request page or contact walfit2026@hotmail.com. Requests are reviewed manually and require safe account-ownership verification. This policy does not promise a fixed response or completion time.

6. Retention and deletion limitations

Account content is not subject to a general automatic-expiry schedule in the account-deletion flow. You can request deletion as described above. Delete Account and Delete Data have different scopes; neither is a promise that every copy of information is erased immediately.

Membership and access records after Delete Data
Delete Data keeps the login and specified membership, subscription, billing-related and access metadata so the retained account can keep its assigned access. Delete Account also targets those records when they are stored inside your private account data.
Limited account-reset security record
Limited account-deletion security information, including an account identifier, reset state and timestamps, may remain outside your private account data to prevent stale sessions from recreating deleted content. No fixed expiration period is specified here, and the account-deletion flow does not automatically remove this record.
Local recovery and reminder information
Limited account identifiers, status, timestamps and reminder settings can remain to complete interrupted cleanup and prevent reminders from restarting. Temporary recovery or staging files may also remain after an interruption and require successful recovery or cleanup before removal; completion of the main recovery record does not guarantee removal of every staging file. The main local recovery record is removed after successful finalization; other reset and notification-suppression information can remain separately.
Older or unverifiable local files
Cleanup removes supported records that can safely be attributed to the account. Files with unknown or ambiguous ownership can remain to avoid erasing another account’s data. Contact support if cleanup cannot finish.
References in other records
References to a coach or administrator in other users’ records, and shared operational records, are outside the automatic private-account cleanup. They require separate assessment without deleting other users’ information.
Account-deletion request emails
Waleed Mohamed retains account-deletion request emails for 90 days after the deletion request is completed, then manually deletes them from the support mailbox. This is a manual operational policy, not an automated app or email-deletion feature. It does not guarantee immediate erasure of copies retained in the email provider’s logs or backups, and does not set a retention period for unrelated support correspondence.
Provider logs and backups
The account-deletion flow does not itself erase provider operational logs, cloud backups or device backups. This policy does not specify a verified deletion schedule for those copies or promise that they disappear immediately.
Other devices and shared copies
Deletion is not a remote wipe of other or offline devices or SDK-managed caches. It cannot recall summaries already shared with recipients or remove original images from your photo library.

Except for the 90-day manual retention policy for completed account-deletion request emails, no fixed retention periods are specified here for remaining security, recovery, unrelated support correspondence or external records. The limitations above describe what automatic deletion does and does not cover; they do not establish a legal obligation or permission to retain information indefinitely. Contact walfit2026@hotmail.com about remaining account-linked information.
